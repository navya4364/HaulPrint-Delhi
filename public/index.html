HaulPrint Delhi

A Geospatial Transport-Emissions Simulator for Solid Waste in Delhi

Measure the carbon footprint of hauling waste

Prototype notice: All inputs are illustrative sample values, and results are calculated from them. This is not official data and the project is not affiliated with any government body. Change any input and every result updates.
How it works: 1. Select a Delhi zone → 2. Enter waste, vehicle and route inputs → 3. All three scenarios are simulated instantly → 4. Compare environmental and economic impact → 5. See the recommended strategy.

Method, limitations and roadmap

  • Distance = baseline km/day × (1 − route distance cut)
  • Fuel = distance ÷ mileage
  • CO₂ = fuel × emission factor (an assumed diesel factor, editable above)
  • Transport cost = fuel × diesel price + distance × other running cost (transport only; excludes the investment in composting units and hubs)
  • Tonne-km = waste hauled (t) × average haul distance (km)

Limitations: static waste weights (no seasonal moisture effects); distances are assumed rather than from live traffic; emission factors are standard estimates; scenario percentages are assumptions to be replaced by routing results.

Backend: choose “Network route model” to send the inputs to a Python (FastAPI) service. It builds a synthetic street grid with NetworkX, compares separate trips with consolidated loops (a travelling-salesman approximation), counts truck trips from load factors, and calculates fuel, CO₂, cost and tonne-km with Pandas. If the service is unavailable, the page falls back to the assumption-based results.

Roadmap: replace the synthetic grid with real road networks and validate against real GPS logs from a pilot zone.
